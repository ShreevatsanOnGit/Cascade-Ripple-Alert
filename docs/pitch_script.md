# CascadeMap: Pitch & Demo Script

## The 2-Minute Pitch Outline

**1. The Hook (0:00 - 0:20)**
"Infrastructure is currently monitored asset by asset. But in a real disaster, a flooded road doesn't just stop cars—it stops fuel trucks reaching generators, which takes down power grids, which knocks out hospitals. One failure becomes many. We built CascadeMap to solve this blind spot."

**2. The Solution (0:20 - 0:50)**
"CascadeMap is an interactive simulation platform for city planners. Instead of looking at static points on a map, we model the city as a living, interdependent network. We use real open-source city data, graph theory to calculate rerouting loads, and an AI engine to translate complex math into actionable situation reports."

**3. The 'Wow' Moment (0:50 - 1:30)**
"Let me show you. (Start Demo). When we knock out this central bridge, you don't just see a red X. You see the cascade. Traffic reroutes, overloading these three arterial roads in Round 2. By Round 3, 12,000 residents are cut off from the nearest hospital. We calculate a live 'Network Resilience Score' and provide a natural-language brief of the exact human impact."

**4. The Close (1:30 - 2:00)**
"CascadeMap moves disaster planning from reactive to proactive. By identifying the exact nodes that trigger catastrophic cascades, planners know exactly where their limited infrastructure budget will save the most lives. Thank you."

## The 60-Second Live Demo Script

*(Prerequisite: App is open, map centered on a dense downtown area)*

1. **(0:00-0:10)** "Here is the real road and infrastructure network of [City Name]. On the left, our dashboard tracks the overall Network Resilience Score."
2. **(0:10-0:25)** "Watch what happens if a flash flood takes out this major intersection. *[Click the intersection -> Click Simulate]*"
3. **(0:25-0:45)** "Notice the timeline scrubber. In Round 1, the intersection fails. As traffic reroutes, watch Round 2—the neighboring bridge hits 120% capacity and collapses under the load. In Round 3, the cascade knocks out access to the City Hospital."
4. **(0:45-1:00)** "Finally, our AI agent instantly generates a situation report based on the math, highlighting that 8,000 people are now stranded. This is how planners can visualize the butterfly effect before a real disaster strikes."
